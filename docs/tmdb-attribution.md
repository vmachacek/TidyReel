# TMDB attribution asset

`apps/pocket_cinema/assets/branding/tmdb.svg` is TMDB's approved **Primary long (blue)** logo. It was downloaded unchanged from TMDB on October 7, 2026:

- [TMDB logos and attribution](https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution)
- [Original official SVG](https://www.themoviedb.org/assets/v4/logos/v2/blue_long_2-9665a76b1ae401a510ec1e0ca40ddcb3b0cfe45f1d51b77a308fea0845885648.svg)
- SVG SHA-256: `9665a76b1ae401a510ec1e0ca40ddcb3b0cfe45f1d51b77a308fea0845885648`, matching the digest in the official asset URL.

`tmdb.png` is a transparent 640 x 46 pixel rasterization of that SVG, generated with Sharp at a density of 300 and a width of 640 pixels. The source artwork, approved colors, orientation, and proportions were retained; no image generation was used.

[TMDB's attribution requirements](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq) require the logo in an About or Credits section, less prominently than the app's own branding, together with this notice:

> This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

The logo identifies TMDB as the metadata source. Its inclusion does not imply endorsement of Pocket Cinema.
